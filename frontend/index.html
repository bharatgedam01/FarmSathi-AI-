What is a Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)?

Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) are agricultural science centers established by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) in every rural district of India. KVK agronomists provide free on-farm testing, certified soil testing, pest diagnosis, and technical training. If your crop has an acute fungal or insect outbreak, take an affected plant specimen inside a clean plastic bag to your district KVK.

Safe Agrochemical Guidelines

  • Wear protective gear (mask, goggles, gloves) when handling any agricultural chemicals.
  • Never spray against the wind direction.
  • Never wash pesticide sprayers in ponds, wells, or drinking water streams.
  • Read the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) label for approved waiting periods before harvest.

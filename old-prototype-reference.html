💧 Irrigation guidance

A simple checklist before watering your field.

🌱
1. Check the crop stage

Seedling, flowering and grain-filling stages can have different water needs.

🪴
2. Check soil moisture

Inspect soil near the root zone; avoid watering only by calendar.

🌧️
3. Consider recent rainfall

Review rain forecasts and drainage to reduce water waste and waterlogging.

🚿
4. Choose a suitable method

Where practical, drip or sprinkler irrigation can help manage water use.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions from Farmers

Everything you need to know about starting with FarmSaathi AI.

Is FarmSaathi AI free to use?

Yes, FarmSaathi AI is completely free for farmers. You can check weather forecasts, analyze crops, view mandi rates, and manage your farm plots at zero cost.

Can I use FarmSaathi AI on my mobile phone?

Absolutely. FarmSaathi AI is fully responsive and optimized for smartphones, tablets, and computers. It works smoothly even on mobile browsers with standard 3G/4G connectivity.

Which languages are supported?

FarmSaathi AI currently supports English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Marathi (मराठी). You can switch languages at any time with one click.

Is my farm data safe and private?

Yes. Using Supabase Row Level Security (RLS), your farmer profile, crops, activities, and scan history are strictly private and accessible only when logged into your account.
